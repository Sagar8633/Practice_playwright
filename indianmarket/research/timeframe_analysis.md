FINAL_H4 = the ATR-scaled exit stack selected in the gold study (BE 2 ATR, trailing from 5 ATR at 0.5 ATR distance, 0.1 ATR step, buffer 0, MA18 exit, swing stop). Both without the volume filter (an index has no volume).
