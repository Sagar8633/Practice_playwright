Heatmap

Instrument (list at the top left): gold, Bitcoin, Ether, Solana and XRP come from Binance futures, moved to the real-dollar price (spot gold, or Coinbase for crypto). EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, USD/CAD and USD/CHF come from Kraken's fiat markets: real dollar prices, but a small market, so a few pips off a broker at times, with no buy / sell volume (no absorption areas, a neutral tape) and 12 hours of 1-minute history. USD/JPY barely trades there and is not offered. Zones, ideas and alerts are kept per instrument. The option bars and the MT5 bridge are for gold only.

Bars on the right (gold only): gamma exposure at each strike, from GLD (gold ETF) options, placed at the matching gold price. Green = calls outweigh puts, red = puts outweigh calls.

GLD Call Wall / Put Wall (dashed): the strike of the nearest expiry holding the most call / put open interest (calls minus puts), within 3 % of the price; none is drawn when there is none that close. CW1-3 / PW1-3 (dotted): the three biggest across all expiries, within 8 % of the price.

GLD Zero Gamma (blue): the price where the nearest expiry's net gamma changes sign.

These option levels are GLD's. They are not the COMEX gold-options levels that paid GEX tools show, and on 6 Nov 2026 the two did not agree (GLD was call-heavy where such a tool showed its put wall). They are context only: signals and MT5 trades do not use them.

Zones (boxes): red = sell zone, the candle that made a swing high on the 30-minute or 1-hour chart; green = buy zone, the candle that made a swing low. A zone turns pale once price has left it and come back, and disappears when a candle of its timeframe closes through it.

Absorption (thin bands): over 5 minutes one side traded much more than usual and price still did not go its way. Blue = heavy selling that buyers absorbed; pink = heavy buying that sellers absorbed. Measured from the buy and sell volume of Binance gold futures, the same tape the heatmap page shows. A band disappears when a 1-minute candle closes through it, or after 8 hours.

Profile: volume traded at each price since 18:00 New York. Yellow = POC, the busiest price. Purple = value area, where 70 % of the volume traded.

Guide (left): the top box says what to do now. WAIT = price is between zones. GET READY = price is at a zone, no entry yet. BUY / SELL = a 5-minute candle went into the zone and closed back out, and the checks agree. NO: SKIP = it turned, but the target is too close, the stop too wide or too little agrees. The checklist shows each point with a tick or a cross. The plan under a zone is rough: the real entry is the close of the candle that turns.

Price alerts: type a price in the guide, hold Alt and click the chart, or right-click the chart. An orange line appears and the alarm rings once when price gets there. Right-click the line to remove it. The price is this page's price, which can differ a little from your broker's.

Alarm: a new BUY or SELL sounds a 30-second siren (high and fast for buy, low and slow for sell), shows a flashing banner and flashes the tab title until you click the banner. A price alert sounds a fast trill, and reaching a zone a short tone. Sound only works after one click on the page, and its loudness is set by the PC volume. Keep this tab open in its own window: a hidden or sleeping tab checks late or not at all.

MT5: with MT5 on, each new signal is passed to the GexSignalTrader EA in MetaTrader 5, which places the trade with the same stop and target distance, closes it after 4 hours, and reports back here. It needs start_gex.bat running, the EA attached to the gold chart and Algo Trading on. The EA refuses to trade on a real account.

Mouse: wheel on the chart zooms time, wheel on the price scale or bars zooms price, drag moves, double-click fits, Alt + click or right-click sets a price alert.

None of this predicts price. Tested on 5 years of gold, the BUY / SELL ideas of this guide made nothing before costs and lost 0.2 to 0.5 of the risk per trade after them (about 560 ideas a year). Absorption areas, tested on 90 days, did no better. Nothing has been tested on the other instruments. The guide is a checklist to keep you out of poor trades, not a signal service.
