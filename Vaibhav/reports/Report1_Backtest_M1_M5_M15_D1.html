The answer first

One timeframe survives, and only with a different exit

Net result of the untouched EA, Sep 2020 to Sep 2026, realistic costs, fixed 0.01 lot. Hover any chart for the numbers; every chart has a table view.

  • M1 and M5 fail on costs, not on signal. Before spread and slippage M1 is +$877 over six years (zero); XM's spread is 56% of the one-minute ATR. Nothing tested is positive in any period.
  • M15 is break-even before costs and a loser after them. Single changes reach zero to +$717, but none is positive in the 2020 to 2023 development window.
  • D1 is real but conditional. The reported 3x reproduces only with the 1% stop filter off; 2025 alone supplies all 23 years of profit. Volatility-scaled protection with an ADX filter roughly triples the six-year result and is positive in every split, yet is flat over 2003 to 2020.
  • Winners become losers because of the $5 break-even stop. On D1 it closed 70 of 85 trades that averaged $37 of open profit; on M15, 62% of losing trades were in profit first.
Step 1 · what the code does

The EA in one pass through a bar

New barSignal check18 SMA above 200 SMA, two closes above both, volume above its 20-bar mean (mirror for sells).
OrderBuy stop10 points above the last high, stop at the last confirmed swing low. No expiry; cancelled only when a close crosses the 18 SMA.
Gate1% ruleThe setup is skipped if the swing-stop loss at 0.01 lot exceeds 1% of balance. On $200 that is any stop wider than $2.00.
Every tickBreak-evenAt +$5.00 the stop moves to entry +$0.10. Tighten-only for the rest of the trade.
Every tickProtectionSwing low minus $0.50 (default), or Chandelier, or fixed trailing; updates only while the trade is at least $5 in profit.
New barMA18 exitMarket close when the completed bar closes through the 18 SMA.
  • The lot is fixed at 0.01. The "1%" input is a gate, not position sizing.
  • Break-even, protection and trailing thresholds are in points and do not scale with timeframe or volatility: 500 points is 5x the M1 ATR in 2021 and a tenth of the D1 ATR in 2026.
  • A buy stop must sit above the ask. On M1 the 40 to 50 point spread rejects about 40% of setups as "invalid price".
  • Failed-breakout exit and partial close are dead code (never called).
  • ADX and session filters exist but ship switched off.
  • One pending order or one position at a time; no take-profit, no time exit.

Step 24 · what to change

EA changes the tests support

  1. Protection: Chandelier from the first tick. ProtectionMode = PROTECTION_CHANDELIER, ProtectionStartMode = START_IMMEDIATELY, lookback 22, ATR 22, multiplier 3.0. No code change. 2020 to 2026: +$619 becomes +$1,435; 2003 to 2026: +$121 becomes +$1,113; better in every 8-year window; stable for lookback 11 to 33 and multiplier 2.25 to 4.5.
  2. Add an ATR-trailing protection mode (stop = best price since entry minus 2 x ATR(22), tighten-only, every tick). 2020 to 2026 +$1,736; 2003 to 2026 +$1,530, and with ADX 25 +$1,808 with 13 of 23 years positive: the most regime-robust exit tested.
  3. Enable the ADX filter on D1: UseADXFilter = true, MinimumADX = 25. With Chandelier +$1,435 becomes +$1,575 and max drawdown $119 becomes $65, positive in all three splits. Small sample (21 to 28 development trades).
  4. Keep break-even at 500 points. 300 or 400 cut the candidate by 40%; removing it entirely is worse in both periods.
  5. Never use 500-point fixed trailing or the TWK trail on D1: -$74 and -$15 over six years against +$619 for the shipped stack.
  6. Keep the 1% gate and make the balance requirement explicit. Median D1 stop is $87 per 0.01 lot, so the rule needs about $8,700. Add a startup check that prints the implied minimum balance instead of silently skipping every setup (370 setups, zero trades, on $200).
  7. Do not add entry filters on D1 and do not change the initial stop: nothing passed the declared rule on 85 trades; the v13-style 1,500-point cap looks good only in 2025.
  8. ATR-scale the point thresholds eventually (the only D1 exit positive in all three splits at +0.15 / +0.31 / +0.30 R was the 1-ATR break-even with 2/1-ATR trailing, but on 54 trades). Forward-test first.
  9. Housekeeping: remove the dead failed-breakout and partial-close code; the stop-percent check runs twice per order; buy stops inside the spread fail with "invalid price".

Not recommended: any deployment on M1, M5 or M15; the New York + volatility + ADX stack on M5 (-$128 over six years, positive only in 2025 to 2026); the volatility filter on M15 (fails the declared rule); any hybrid beyond the components above.

Method and files

How the numbers were made

  • Engine: an exact Python port of the MQL5 logic run bar by bar on M1 bars (H1 before Sep 2020) in XM server time. Entries and the MA18 exit run on the first bar of each signal bar with the two completed bars, as in OnTick; break-even, protection, trailing and stop hits are evaluated on every M1 bar with the bar's extreme as best price.
  • MT5 order validity is modelled: a buy stop must be above the ask, a sell stop below the bid, a stop modification on the correct side of the market; failures are retried like the real EA.
  • Intrabar ordering: existing stop first, then fills, then stop moves; a stop moved inside a bar is tested against the close. The "worst" ordering changes D1 by $9 and M15 by $1,700 (see the baseline note).
  • Prices are Dukascopy's feed, not XM's: closes differ by 5 cents at the median and the volume filters agree on 82% of bars, so the MT5 tester will differ trade by trade.
  • Every run is in results/experiment_log.csv; the written report is FINAL_REPORT.md; trade-level data in results/trade_analysis/ and results/research_tables.xlsx; all in Vaibhav/research/.
