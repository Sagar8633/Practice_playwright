Result

C. No exploitable mechanism found in 28 event families. Three gross asymmetries exist; none pays the spread.

110 ledger rows (28 hypotheses, traded with and against their event direction, plus first-of-day and M15 variants): 94 rejected in development, 16 with too few events, none weak or candidate. The follow-up on the strongest gross asymmetries, with wider units and longer holds, found no shape or horizon that is net positive in development on at least 200 events.

What the search did find is worth stating precisely. The first 30 minutes of the Asian session predict the next hours (gross PF 1.15, 1.14, 1.10 in the three periods at 1 ATR; 1.29, 1.44, 1.15 at 1:2), one event per day, and the spread at 02:00 server time takes it all back. Continuation after an exhaustion bar and the adverse timing of the TWK flip are the same story at smaller scale. Two more (fading the first expansion out of a low-volatility regime, continuation after an Asian-range sweep) hold in development and validation and vanish out of sample: regime effects, not mechanisms.

1. Result by family

  • Volatility (H01 to H04). Compression breaks, shock bars and the first expansion after a quiet regime all lose with the event and show no stable asymmetry against it; the expansion-out-of-compression fade (gross +0.11R DEV, +0.29R VAL, -0.01R OOS) is the one that fooled development and validation together.
  • Liquidity and range (H05 to H11). Previous-day sweeps, breaks, retests, Asian and London range breaks, opening-range breaks, equal-high sweeps: gross within 0.05R of zero in every period except the Asian-sweep continuation (gross +0.07R DEV, +0.11R VAL, -0.02R OOS). Session ranges on gold at these timeframes are not levels the market respects in a tradeable way.
  • Mean reversion (H12 to H14). Fading a 3-ATR displacement from VWAP has the most significant z-score in the ledger (2.7) and the smallest effect (+0.015R gross): with 89,000 events, significance is cheap and money is not. The 20-bar z-score and the gap from the previous close are the same.
  • Momentum (H15 to H17). Five candles in a row, displacement-retrace-reclaim and break-retest-hold sequences: no continuation tendency in either direction beyond noise.
  • Exhaustion (H18 to H20). Wick rejections at the day's extreme and third touches do not reverse. Fading a climax bar loses; continuing after it is gross-positive in all three periods (+0.005R, +0.05R, +0.18R at 1:2) and net-positive only out of sample.
  • Time of day (H21). The London and New York first-30-minute directions carry nothing. The Asian one does, gross, in every period, and is the least executable event in the set.
  • TWK as something else (H22 to H24). Contrarian: gross +0.02R to +0.06R in development and validation, zero out of sample, -0.16R to -0.41R net. The flip-adverse-reclaim sequence loses (-0.46R net DEV). The first flip of the day is no better than the rest.
  • State transitions (H25 to H28). Compression to expansion, expansion to retracement, breakout to failure: rejected. Trend to exhaustion has too few events (428) and no net edge either way.
  • First versus later occurrence. In no family does the first event of the day behave differently enough from later ones to change a verdict; the differences are within the noise of a few hundred events.

  • Statistical test. z-score of the gross hit rate against its break-even (50% for symmetric shapes) and a bootstrap 95% interval of the net expectancy; both reported, neither alone decides.
  • Split. Development Sep 2021 to Dec 2023 for discovery; validation 2024 untouched until a hypothesis is frozen; out of sample Jan 2025 to Sep 2026 final; per-year walk-forward for anything not rejected.
  • Acceptance. At least 200 development events, 50 validation, 100 out of sample; net expectancy above zero and profit factor above 1 in all three periods on the primary shape or any single fixed shape; then years positive, cost at 1.25x, 1.5x and 2x, no month above 40% of net, no trade above 20%, both directions and no single session carrying it. 10. What this rules out, and what is left

  • Ruled out at XM's spread on M3 to M15 gold: volatility-regime breaks and shocks, session-range breaks and sweeps, opening ranges, previous-day levels, equal highs and lows, VWAP and z-score reversion, candle runs, displacement sequences, wick rejections, climax fades, touch counts, session-open momentum, compression-expansion-breakout-failure transitions, the TWK flip as entry, contrarian, sequence or first-of-day signal. Each was measured, not assumed.
  • A real but uneconomic asymmetry: Asian-open continuation. If you ever trade an instrument or account where the spread at that hour is under a tenth of the 5-minute ATR (gold futures, or a raw-spread account at a quieter hour of a different session), it is the first thing to re-test. On XM standard it is dead on arrival.
  • Not measurable here: news reactions (no calendar), order-flow behaviour (no book), sub-minute behaviour (no ticks), and your own selection (no log). Those are the datasets in which an edge could still live; nothing in five years of bars at this cost level contains one.
  • The engine is reusable. Any new event is a function returning (bar index, side); the ledger, the split, the tests and the follow-up run unchanged. New data (a calendar, ticks, your trades) plugs in as new event families.

11. Method and limits

  • Events are evaluated on M5 (M3 for the TWK families) with an M15 cross-check reserved for anything reaching WEAK; nothing did.
  • Sessions and opening ranges use server time (EET). Daily rollover at 00:00 server; the Asian range is 01:00 to 09:59, London 10:00 to 14:59, New York 15:00 to 23:59.
  • The primary shape is a 1 ATR stop with a 2 ATR target; the directional shape is 1 ATR each way; 0.25 and 0.5 ATR shapes are in the ledger CSV. Ties inside a one-minute bar count as the stop.
  • The 'drift to horizon' rows in the follow-up (hold with no stop for 100 or 300 bars) are reported for completeness; their variance is enormous and their sign flips between periods, which is why they are not evidence of anything.
  • Limits: 39 of 61 months; bar paths not ticks; yearly median spread; no slippage beyond the cost multipliers.
