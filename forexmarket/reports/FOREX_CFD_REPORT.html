Method and files

How the numbers were made

  • Engine: the gold research engine (exact port of the MQL5 v1.00 logic, validated against the MT5 tester) generalised to any instrument: point, contract, swap points and triple-swap day are parameters; it reproduces the gold study's numbers to the cent on the gold data.
  • Path: one-minute Dukascopy bid candles in XM server time; signals on completed bars, stops and fills on the next minute's range; MT5 order validity modelled; H1 2015-2021 for the long D1 test.
  • Costs: XM's own spread by hour and year, slippage per side, XM swap points, USD conversion at the exit price for USD-base pairs.
  • Every run is logged in research/experiment_log.csv; every trade list is in backtests/<instrument>/; the written reports are in reports/.
