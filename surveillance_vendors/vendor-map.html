How to read this

Segment tags describe what a company actually ships, not what its marketing claims. Most serious players cover two or three — a camera maker with an analytics suite, or a VMS vendor with an ANPR module.

The India ops tag marks global companies with real engineering, manufacturing or delivery teams in India, not just a sales office.

Where to track it

  • IPVM — the industry's independent testing and news desk; the single best source on who is real.
  • NIST FRVT / FATE — the neutral scoreboard for face-recognition accuracy claims.
  • Trade shows — ISC West (US), IFSEC (UK), Secutech & IFSEC India, Intersec Dubai, Security China.
  • Tenders — Indian smart-city and safe-city ICCC bids on GeM and state portals reveal the real integrator landscape.

Caveats

This sector consolidates constantly — Canon, Motorola Solutions, Hanwha and Axon have each absorbed several names below. Ownership and status reflect what was public as of early 2026; verify before you rely on any single entry.
