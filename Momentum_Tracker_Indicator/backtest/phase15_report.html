XAUUSD, phase 15, post-release microstructure transition

Post-Release Transition

After the 15:30 server liquidity shock has passed, is there a measurable and executable transition into directional price discovery? Dukascopy ticks, 95 Thursday/Friday releases of 2025 (development only; 2026 untouched), executable quotes throughout.

Classification

C. After the quote normalises, the post-release window is statistically the same as a same-day control window: no direction, no excess volatility, nothing executable.

The transition the brief describes exists and is measurable. The Dukascopy quote at 15:30:00 opens 2.3 times its normal width, peaks at 3.7 times inside the first second, and returns: below 2x normal after 5 seconds at the median, below 1.5x after 20 seconds, below 1.25x after 25 seconds, with a long tail (one event in four takes more than two minutes to reach 1.25x, one in ten more than four and a half minutes). Thursdays normalise more slowly than Fridays. The price, meanwhile, keeps moving: at the normalisation time only a quarter of the 30-minute displacement has happened, at 60 seconds a third, at 5 minutes two thirds. So the premise held: most of the post-release move happens with a normal spread.

The information did not. From the moment the spread has normalised, the probability of reaching +0.5 ATR before -0.5 ATR is 0.47 to 0.55 whichever way you look (either side, either weekday, 1 to 60 minutes), and the same for 1 ATR. Five shock states that could plausibly carry direction (displacement at normalisation, 5-second return, tick imbalance in the first 5 and next 25 seconds, which side of the quote moved) all give hit rates whose confidence intervals contain 0.5, and executable hit rates of 0.22 to 0.30. The first clean move continues 45 to 49 percent of the time and its mean 30-minute close is zero. Delayed momentum over 15 to 120 seconds with 30 to 60 minute holds is gross-positive by 0.4 to 0.7 ATR, but the identical rule at 16:30 and 17:15 on the same days is gross-positive by 0.5 ATR too, no cell reaches |t| = 2, and the pre-declared plateau rule retained nothing. That is 2025 gold drifting in the US session, not the release.

Track B fails the same way. From the normalisation time the market reaches 1 ATR in 138 seconds against 173 for the controls, and the 30-minute range is 3.5 ATR against 3.7: the release's extra volatility is spent inside the shock, where Phase 14 showed it cannot be bought. Every two-sided design placed after the shock (OCO breakouts at 0.25 to 0.75 ATR, the first-minute range breakout) is negative at one spread, worse at 1.25x, and within noise of the same rule at the control times. The pullback candidate was not built because its precondition failed. Outcome C, on development data only; 2026 was never read.

Two things stand out in the band table. First, the shock is a spread and displacement event, not a tick-count event: the tick rate is 5 to 10 per second in every band, including the first 100 milliseconds. Second, the tick direction is one-sided only while the quote is wide: the absolute tick imbalance is 1.0 in the first half second, 0.15 at 2 to 5 seconds and 0.07 from 10 seconds on, which is the level of a normal window. By the time a retail order could be placed with a normal spread, the order flow is already balanced.
