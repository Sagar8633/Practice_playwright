Heatmap

Instrument (list at the top left): gold, Bitcoin, Ether, Solana and XRP come from Binance futures, moved to the real-dollar price (spot gold, or Coinbase for crypto). EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, USD/CAD and USD/CHF come from Kraken's fiat markets: real dollar prices, but a small market, so a few pips off a broker at times, with no buy / sell volume (no absorption areas, a neutral tape) and 12 hours of 1-minute history. USD/JPY barely trades there and is not offered. Zones, ideas and alerts are kept per instrument. The option bars and the MT5 bridge are for gold only.

Bars on the right (gold only): gamma exposure at each strike, from GLD (gold ETF) options, placed at the matching gold price. Green = calls outweigh puts, red = puts outweigh calls. With Call / Put OI selected, each strike shows two bars instead: green = call open interest, red = put open interest.

GLD Call Wall / Put Wall (dashed): the strike of the nearest expiry holding the most call / put open interest (calls minus puts), within 3 % of the price; none is drawn when there is none that close. CW1-3 / PW1-3 (dotted): the three biggest across all expiries, within 8 % of the price.

GLD Zero Gamma (blue): the price where the nearest expiry's net gamma changes sign.

These option levels are GLD's. They are not the COMEX gold-options levels that paid GEX tools show, and on 6 Nov 2026 the two did not agree (GLD was call-heavy where such a tool showed its put wall). They are context only: signals and MT5 trades do not use them.

Zones (boxes): red = sell zone, the candle that made a swing high on the 30-minute or 1-hour chart; green = buy zone, the candle that made a swing low. A zone turns pale once price has left it and come back, and disappears when a candle of its timeframe closes through it.

Absorption (thin bands): over 5 minutes one side traded much more than usual and price still did not go its way. Blue = buyers absorbed; pink = sellers absorbed. Measured from the buy and sell volume of Binance gold futures, the same tape the heatmap page shows. A band disappears when a 1-minute candle closes through it, or after 8 hours.

TWK (button): toggles the trade_with_kareena Tracker overlay and its multi-timeframe volume / R-Factor table. It uses this page's candles and selected timeframe, so values can differ from TradingView's feed. NSE:NIFTY is not available as a benchmark here, so Sector Strength is neutral (0). Its buy / sell rows classify volume by candle direction, and its "OI / Derivatives" formula uses volume change, not open-interest data; bubbles mark relative candle volume, not participant identity. The on / off choice is saved in this browser.

TWK live position and alerts: the latest valid 1:2 position box extends through the current bar and stays until a new valid LONG / SHORT replaces it. A confirmed 15-minute Smart Trail flip uses the page's Alarm button; this alert works even when the TWK overlay is hidden. With MT5 on, a new closed 15-minute signal with ADX(14,14) above 20 opens 0.01 lots. An opposite qualifying signal closes the open TWK trade and opens the new side; same-direction signals do not add. Stop, target, or an opposite qualifying signal exits the trade. Old signals are not replayed when the page opens.

Trade taken alert: after MT5 confirms an order as placed, a large popup appears and a separate call-style ringtone repeats until you silence it or press Esc. The popup has its own saved volume slider. Use Trade sound test to hear it without sending an order. A signal alone does not trigger this alert.

Profile: volume traded at each price since 18:00 New York. Yellow = POC, the busiest price. Purple = value area, where 70 % of the volume traded. Built on fixed 0.5 $ rows from closed 1-minute candles, so zooming does not move POC, VAH or VAL, and the guide uses the same POC. When the POC moves 2 $ or more and stays there for two minutes, a "POC SHIFTED" banner appears (with a tone when the alarm is on); switch it off under Price alerts in the guide.

Past days (gold and crypto): the chart holds the last 30 trading days (18:00 to 18:00 New York) of Binance 1-minute candles, kept in this browser; a day older than 30 is deleted. The Past days on / off button hides or shows previous days' POC, value-area paths, expired zone history and day-start lines. Active buy / sell zones stay visible even if they formed on an earlier day. A dashed line marks where each day starts. The yellow step line is how that day's POC moved, the thin purple lines its value area; the dots mark the POC at the end of Asia (03:00 New York), the London morning (08:30) and the close (17:00), each from the candles up to then only. Past buy / sell zones are drawn from the swing candle to the moment a candle closed through them (or a newer zone took over). Go back with the day list at the top (or the arrow keys), by dragging the chart, or with the scroll bar under the time scale. The Days tab of the left panel lists the day's numbers, its POC shifts and its zones, the last 30 days in one list, and downloads them as CSV. Prices are moved to spot with today's futures-to-spot shift, so an older day can sit 1-2 $ off the spot price it had then. Forex has no history: Kraken gives 12 hours only.

Guide (left): the top box says what to do now. The stop of an idea is 1 x the 5-minute ATR beyond the zone or the turning candle, at least 2 $ on gold. WAIT = price is between zones. GET READY = price is at a zone, no entry yet. BUY / SELL = a 5-minute candle went into the zone and closed back out, and the checks agree. NO: SKIP = it turned, but the target is too close, the stop too wide or too little agrees. The checklist shows each point with a tick or a cross. The plan under a zone is rough: the real entry is the close of the candle that turns.

Price alerts: type a price in the guide, hold Alt and click the chart, or right-click the chart. An orange line appears and the alarm rings once when price gets there. Right-click the line to remove it. The price is this page's price, which can differ a little from your broker's.

Alarm: a new BUY or SELL sounds a 30-second siren (high and fast for buy, low and slow for sell), shows a flashing banner and flashes the tab title until you click the banner. A price alert sounds a fast trill, and reaching a zone a short tone. Sound only works after one click on the page, and its loudness is set by the PC volume. Keep this tab open in its own window: a hidden or sleeping tab checks late or not at all.

TWK auto: when enabled, only a new, closed 15-minute TWK LONG / SHORT signal with ADX(14,14) above 20 can be sent to MetaTrader 5, at fixed 0.01 lots. GEX guide signals remain visible but never place orders. The local agent and GexSignalTrader EA must be running on a demo account with Algo Trading on. The EA reports order results here. Old signals are not replayed when the page opens.

Price (button): the candles are Binance's gold future, moved down to a spot price. Price: gold-api uses gold-api.com, the only free spot price a browser may read; it runs about 90 seconds late and can sit 1-2 $ off a broker. With start_gex.bat running, a click on the button takes the price from this PC instead: your MetaTrader 5 gold chart's bid (GexSignalTrader 1.08 attached; button shows the symbol), or otherwise Swissquote's live spot (button shows Swissquote), both within a few tenths of your broker or TradingView. Without the agent, match it under Price alerts takes the price your own chart shows and keeps that difference for 8 hours. The candles keep Binance's shape and volume.

Market hours (gold): gold trades Sunday 18:00 to Friday 17:00 New York, with a break from 17:00 to 18:00 every day. Binance's gold future also trades in between; those candles are left out everywhere, the weekend shows as a grey band, and while gold is closed the page says MARKET CLOSED and does nothing. Holidays are not known to the page.

Panels: drag the inner edge of the guide (left) or the option bars (right) to make it wider or narrower; drag it most of the way in and let go to minimise it. A minimised panel leaves an arrow tab at the screen edge: click it, or drag it back out, to open the panel again. A click on an arrow tab opens or closes its panel; a double-click on the edge restores the default width. Full screen hides both and fills the screen; Esc or the button again puts them back. A hidden guide still records ideas, sounds the alarm, draws the arrows and sends to MT5: switch those with the Alarm and MT5 buttons.

Mouse: wheel on the chart zooms time, wheel on the price scale or bars (or Shift + wheel) zooms price, drag moves, double-click or Fit fits the candles (levels outside the screen are then pinned to its top or bottom edge with an arrow), Alt + click or right-click sets a price alert. On a phone: one finger pans, two fingers pinch to zoom time, double-tap fits; the Guide opens over the chart and closes with its Close button.

None of this predicts price. Tested on 5 years of gold, the BUY / SELL ideas of this guide made nothing before costs and lose after them: with the stop used since 11 Nov 2026 (1 x ATR beyond the level, at least 2 $) about 230 ideas a year at −0.08 to −0.20 of the risk per trade after costs; with the old tight stop it was −0.2 to −0.5, and 19 % of its stops were hit before price went on to the target. Absorption areas, tested on 90 days, did no better. Nothing has been tested on the other instruments. The guide is a checklist to keep you out of poor trades, not a signal service.
