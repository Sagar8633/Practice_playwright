What the numbers mean

Businesses found
every businesses row for the campaign, whatever its status
Researched
at least one research_runs row with status = COMPLETE
Qualified
research complete, a current opportunity score, score >= campaigns.min_opportunity_score (70), status not SKIPPED/REJECTED
Skipped
status IN (SKIPPED, REJECTED); the tooltip splits pipeline skips from your rejections
Requiring verification
status IN (AI_RESEARCHED, NEEDS_VERIFICATION)
Ready for outreach
status = CONTACT_READY: verified and a human-confirmed contact exists
Em dash
not measured. A zero means we looked and the answer was none. They are never rendered the same way.
