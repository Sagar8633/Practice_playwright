Classification

C. Historical artifact. The tick reconstruction removes the apparent straddle edge; the range expansion is real but not capturable by stop orders at these distances.

The bar-level result came from three things the one-minute bars could not show: the spread at the release, the order in which the two stops are reached, and which side of the quote each stop lives on. At 15:30:00 server the Dukascopy spread is 0.86 ATR at the median, against a normal 0.25 ATR and a trigger distance of 0.25 ATR. The buy stop therefore sits inside the ask at the moment it is placed and fills on the first tick (median 98 ms), and the bid, which is where both the sell trigger and the long's stop live, is already at or below the sell level. Result on the 86 Thursday and Friday releases of 2025 downloaded: both sides touched in 92% of events, stop hit in 77%, net -0.76 ATR per event, and a mid-to-mid gross of -0.01 ATR, so there is no edge for costs to eat. The random same-day control loses less than the event.

Nothing in the frozen sensitivity set survives on development: triggers from 0.15 to 0.40 ATR lose 0.78 to 0.45 ATR per event (monotone with distance, never positive), every target from 0.5 to 1.5 ATR loses, every holding period gives the same number because the trade is decided in the first minute, and delaying the placement by 30 to 300 seconds after the release still loses, least at 120 seconds. The verdict is reached on development alone; the 2026 hold-out was not consulted (the chronological download had not reached it when this page was built), which the protocol permits for a rejection and forbids for an acceptance.

What is real: the first-minute bid-only range is 1.2 ATR at the median and the spread returns to normal within minutes. That is Report F's phenomenon, confirmed at tick level. It cannot be monetised by a symmetric stop straddle at any distance the plateau covered, because the instrument the straddle needs (a quote that is narrow at the moment of the move) does not exist at 8:30 New York on this feed, and on XM's feed the quoted spread is at least as wide.

This feed is an ECN aggregate, not XM's quote; XM's quoted spread at 15:30 in its own bars is 51 to 70 points, but a bar spread column cannot show a 300 ms spike, and no retail broker quotes a narrow spread at 8:30 New York.

Up-first at 90% is not directional information: it is the buy stop being pre-triggered by the widened ask. The conditional outcome after the first touch is a loss whichever side it is. The straddle therefore has neither directional nor usable volatility information at these distances; the volatility is real but arrives with a spread wider than the trigger.

12. Next step

  • Per the brief, outcome C: reject and return to the remaining missing-information hypotheses. No filter, wider trigger or delayed start is promoted from this study; the delayed-start row is recorded in the ledger as negative on development.
  • What the phenomenon still permits, as hypotheses for a later ledger entry, not as rescues: a directional bet on the release surprise (needs the calendar, Report D), or a limit-order design that provides liquidity into the spike instead of taking it; both are different mechanisms with their own frozen specs, not variants of this one.
  • The manual trade log remains the highest-value unmeasured dataset and needs no infrastructure.
  • Housekeeping: the tick files stay on D: (data/ticks) for the Report E microstructure work; the downloader script and the reconstruction are reusable for any other event window by changing the hours.
