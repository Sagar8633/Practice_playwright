The honest headline

Across every market and timeframe, the average win rate is ~32.7% — essentially sitting on the 33.3% break-even line for a 1:2 target. In plain terms: as-is, this system has no reliable edge. Only 7 of 27 tested combinations were net-profitable, and they cluster in one place — Gold & GBP/EUR on higher timeframes. Fast intraday (5m/15m) is the worst.

What the data says

 

01

No edge as-is. The average win rate (~32.7%) lands right on the 1:2 break-even (33.3%). A mechanical trail-flip with a fixed far target is, by nature, a coin-flip once you pay for it.

02

Higher timeframe = better; faster = worse. 5m & 15m are almost all red. The few winners live on 4h and 1D. Fast intraday amplifies the whipsaws the strategy is prone to, and costs eat a larger slice of each small move.

03

The only real pocket: Gold & GBP/EUR on higher TFs. XAUUSD (PF 1.17–1.33), GBPUSD (1.07–1.12) and EURUSD (1.25 daily) are the consistently non-losing cells. If anything here is worth developing, it's this.

04

Crypto is the worst — especially BTC daily (PF 0.32, −66% drawdown). A two-sided trail strategy gets run over by Bitcoin's long-term uptrend; the shorts bleed for years.

Read before trading a rupee on this

 

  • Backtest ≠ future. These show how the rule would have done on past data. They do not predict future returns.
  • Real costs are usually worse. Modelled at 0.02% + 2 ticks; live broker spreads on gold, indices and crypto CFDs are wider, which pushes thin edges toward zero.
  • Small samples. Several intraday tests have 23–137 trades over weeks — indicative, not conclusive.
  • Windows aren't uniform. Daily results span up to 24 years; intraday spans weeks. Compare within a timeframe, not across.
  • This tests the trail-flip logic, not candlestick patterns — and the simplified R-Factor (no OI/sector) used for the strategy engine.

The one experiment worth running next

 

The data points to a single fix: cut the counter-trend whipsaw trades. Turn on “Require R-Factor confirmation” (only long when R-Factor > 0, short when < 0), or add a 200-EMA trend filter (longs only above it). This usually lifts the win rate a few points and — critically — fixes the BTC-daily disaster by killing the doomed counter-trend shorts. Re-running the whole matrix with that filter on is the highest-value next test.
